Lead-time adjustment tiers

In this mode, farther dates increase the recommended price. The final recommendation combines lead time and occupancy adjustments.
Min lead days Max lead days Adjustment %
Tip: Keep tiers contiguous (e.g., 0–7, 8–30, 31–90, ...).

Forecast occupancy adjustment tiers

In forecast modes, the recommendation uses forecasted occupancy (from the Forecast page heuristic) instead of current occupancy.
Min forecast occ % Max forecast occ % Adjustment %
Tip: Keep tiers contiguous (e.g., 0–35, 36–45, 46–69, ...).

Pricing restrictions

Hard constraints to keep generated prices within acceptable boundaries. Saved locally in this browser.

1) Absolute price bounds (by room type)

Define minimum and maximum allowed prices for each room type. Each constraint can be enabled or disabled.
Enabled Room type Min price (IDR) Max price (IDR)

2) Room type price gap constraints

Ensure price differences between two room types stay within a range (same date & rate plan). Starts empty.
Enabled Room A Room B Min gap (A − B) Max gap (A − B)
No room gap constraints. Click “Add constraint” to create one.

3) Rate plan price gap constraints

Ensure price differences between two rate plans stay within a range (same date & room type). Starts empty.
Enabled Plan A Plan B Min gap (A − B) Max gap (A − B)
No rate plan gap constraints. Click “Add constraint” to create one.
Notes: These are strong constraints applied after rule-based recommendation. When multiple constraints apply, the allowed range is intersected.
